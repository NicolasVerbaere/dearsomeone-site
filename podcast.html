How an episode unfolds

Heard first. Then turned over.

  1. You write in. A struggle, a question, a blessing or something that hurt. Named or anonymous.
  2. We sit with it. The hard part gets heard first. Nothing is rushed or explained away.
  3. We turn it over. Gently, together, sometimes with a guest, we look for what else is true.
  4. You leave with both. Not a fix. A wider view.
