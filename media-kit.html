01 · About

Hi dear someone,

I've been writing since 2018, starting with The Cotton Candy, a mental-health blog that taught me how healing honesty can be. Today I write, speak and create for a community of more than 235,000 people who come for meaning, not trends.

I tell slow, honest stories about faith, emotional well-being, reading and writing, and the quiet work of becoming. For brands, I'm not a billboard. I'm a narrative partner.

— Marlène

What I talk about
Faith & Christian livingMental & emotional well-beingHealing & becomingReading & writingSlow livingWomanhoodLove & relationshipsIdentity — mixed, not confused

Who follows me

Young adults, mostly 18–34, who are values-driven, faith-curious and emotionally literate. They save, share and write back. They don't follow for trends; they follow for meaning.

Where they are

A strong East African core (Burundi, Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, Uganda) and a growing diaspora audience in Canada and beyond. I create in English, French and Kirundi.
