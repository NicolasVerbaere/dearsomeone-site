Terms, kindly put.

Using this site

Welcome! You're free to browse, read and share links to anything here. Please be kind in anything you send us.

Our words & images

All writing, card prompts, photographs, the Dear Someone name and logo are © Marlène Mutwa unless noted otherwise. Please don't copy, reprint or sell them without written permission. Sharing a quote with credit and a link is always welcome. Stories and notes on The Wall belong to the people who wrote them.

The Wall

  • Share only your own story. Leave out other people's full names, and anything else that would let someone be recognised.
  • Be kind. No hate, harassment, explicit content, advertising or links to sell things.
  • Every story and note is read before it goes up. We may decline one, remove a name or detail that could identify someone, or take a note down later. We don't always explain why, and we always try to be gentle about it.
  • Your story stays yours. By pinning it, you let Dear Someone show it on The Wall. You can ask us to take it down at any time by writing to info.dearsomeone@gmail.com.
  • The Wall is not a crisis service. Notes are read when Marlène can, not in real time. If you're in danger, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away.

Not professional advice

Dear Someone talks about mental and emotional health from lived experience and faith. It is not a substitute for therapy, medical care or crisis support. If you are in danger or crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away.
